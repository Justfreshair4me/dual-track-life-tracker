Settings

Local storage & privacy

Notes and audio stay in this browser on this device. Personal and Work have separate histories. They are not encrypted or protected by a separate app lock.

Clearing browser data, losing your device, or removing the app may erase your memories. Browser storage is not automatically backed up. Export a backup regularly and keep a copy outside this device.

This helps reduce automatic eviction; it is not a backup.

Backup & restore

Backups include both Personal and Work, complete text and metadata, and original audio. Files stay on your device. Backups are not encrypted.

No backup export has been requested in this browser.

Choose a .dtbackup file from Downloads or Files. Review it before merging or replacing. Close other app tabs while restoring. Supported limits: 2 GB, 10,000 entries, and 8 MB of text/metadata; your device may have less space. Keep this tab open while checking recordings.

Install & offline use

Open the app once while online and let it finish loading. The production app can then reopen offline at the same address. Microphone recording requires HTTPS or localhost and your permission.

On Android Chrome, open the ⋮ menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” when available.
